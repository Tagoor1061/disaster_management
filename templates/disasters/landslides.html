Municipal Slope Protocols
  • 24/7 Slope Movement Watch: Crack gauges and tilt sensors monitored across vulnerable hill zones.
  • Rainfall Threshold Alerts: Automatic warnings when 64.5 mm/day slide-watch levels are breached.
  • Drainage Clearing Teams: Pre-monsoon desilting of hillside stormwater channels and culverts.
  • Cut-Slope Inspections: Periodic geotechnical review of road-cut and construction slopes.
Relief & Shelter Centers
  • Community halls and municipal schools on high ground prepared as temporary relief shelters.
  • Provision of clean drinking water, food packets, and medical health kits.
  • Emergency rescue units stationed near Kotappakonda and Palnadu foothill villages.
  • Post-slide health camps for injury triage and water-borne disease prevention.

Landslide Safety Guidelines for Citizens

1. Before Intense Rain

Clear drains around hillside homes, check retaining walls for cracks, and identify your nearest uphill assembly point. Keep an emergency grab-bag ready.

2. During a Slide Warning

Evacuate horizontally away from the slope path — never run downhill directly ahead of moving debris. Listen for rumbling sounds and watch for tilting trees or poles.

3. After a Landslide

Stay away from the slide area — secondary slides are common. Check for injured or trapped neighbours, report damage via our portal, and avoid crossing debris-choked roads.
