Wind Speed Warning Scale
  • 30–50 km/h (Strong Breeze): Difficult to use umbrellas; weak tree branches break.
  • 51–75 km/h (High Wind / Gale): Structural damage to loose tin roofs and hoardings.
  • 76–100 km/h (Severe Gale): Trees uprooted; major power line outages expected.
  • >100 km/h (Storm Force): Widespread destruction to power towers and communication lines.
Emergency Removal & Power Ops
  • Disaster response teams equipped with chainsaws for quick tree removal.
  • Electricity Board (APSPDCL) automated feeder trips to prevent electrocution hazards.
  • Removal of illegal or unsafe advertising hoardings along major roads.
  • Traffic rerouting away from high-wind flyovers during squalls.

High Wind Safety Actions for Residents

1. Secure Property

Anchor or bring indoors loose outdoor furniture, potted plants, and tin roof sheets before squalls arrive.

2. Avoid Danger Zones

Do not park cars or stand under large trees, utility poles, or tall billboards during high wind squalls.

3. Report Hazards

Report fallen trees, snapped electrical wires, or hazardous billboards immediately to GMC or via our portal.
