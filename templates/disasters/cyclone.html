Warning Categories & Stages
  • Pre-Cyclone Watch: Issued 72 hours prior to storm onset.
  • Cyclone Alert: Issued 48 hours in advance for coastal districts.
  • Cyclone Warning: Issued 24 hours before expected landfall.
  • Post-Landfall Outlook: Guidance on inland high wind propagation.

Cyclone Survival Actions & Emergency Kit Guide

Mandatory Emergency Survival Kit Checklist

✔️ Drinking water (3 Liters/person/day for 3 days)
✔️ Non-perishable dry food & canned goods
✔️ Battery-operated torch with spare batteries
✔️ First-aid kit & essential daily medications
✔️ Fully charged power banks & battery radio
✔️ Whistle, rope, multi-tool knife & waterproof pouch for IDs

1. Before Cyclone (Pre-Storm Action)

Secure loose roof sheets, trim weak tree branches, stock emergency kit, charge all communication devices, and know your nearest designated cyclone shelter route.

2. During Cyclone (Active Storm Safety)

Stay indoors in the strongest part of your home away from windows and glass doors. Disconnect electrical mains and gas supply. Move to an official shelter immediately if instructed.

3. After Cyclone (Post-Storm Recovery)

Beware of fallen electrical wires and loose poles. Do not enter damaged structures until certified safe. Drink boiled water and report tree falls or power hazards to GMC.
