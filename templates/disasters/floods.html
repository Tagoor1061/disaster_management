Flood Control Command Center

🌧️ Floods & Urban Inundation Management

Real-time river level tracking, drainage canal monitoring, automated flood risk mapping, and municipal emergency de-watering operations for Guntur Corporation.

Municipal Flood Protocols
  • 24/7 Pump Station Operations: Heavy duty de-watering pumps active at all low-lying areas.
  • Canal Clearing Teams: Automated desilting and trash-rack clearing across major city drains.
  • Krishna Barrage Discharge Watch: Inflow & outflow discharge rate monitoring.
  • Low-Lying Area Warnings: Automated SMS alerts to residents in flood-prone wards.
Relief & Shelter Centers
  • Community halls and municipal schools prepared as temporary flood relief shelters.
  • Provision of clean drinking water, food packets, and medical health kits.
  • Emergency boat rescue units stationed near Krishna riverbank villages.
  • Health camps for water-borne disease prevention after flood subsidence.

Flood Safety Guidelines for Citizens

1. Flood Warning Phase

Move valuables, food supplies, and electrical equipment to higher floors. Turn off main gas and electricity switches if water enters home.

2. Active Inundation

Never drive or wade through flooded streets or open drains. Fast-moving water as shallow as 6 inches can sweep you off your feet.

3. Post-Flood Recovery

Boil drinking water before consumption. Report clogged culverts, waterlogging, or damaged pipelines immediately via our portal.
