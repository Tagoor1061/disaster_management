Natural Warning Signs
  • Undersea Earthquake: Strong coastal tremors lasting more than 20 seconds.
  • Rapid Sea Recession: Sudden, unusual drop in sea level exposing the ocean floor.
  • Roaring Ocean Sound: Loud roaring sound coming from the deep sea.
  • Abnormal Waves: Unusually high, rapid cresting wave series approaching shore.
High-Ground Evacuation
  • Immediately move inland or to elevated ground at least 15–30 meters above sea level.
  • Do NOT stay on beaches to watch approaching tsunami waves.
  • Follow designated blue evacuation signs along coastal highways and fishing villages.
  • Stay away from river mouths and coastal streams flowing into the ocean.
Tsunami Helplines
INCOIS Tsunami Center: 040-23895006
State Emergency Ops (SDMA): 1070
Marine Police Control Room: 1093
GMC Disaster Helpline: 1800-425-0001

Tsunami Emergency Response Guidelines

1. Immediate Action

If you feel a severe coastal earthquake or see sudden sea drawdown, head inland immediately without waiting for official warnings.

2. During Tsunami Wave

Remember that a tsunami consists of a series of waves over several hours. The first wave is rarely the largest or most destructive.

3. Post-Tsunami Recovery

Stay on high ground until official "ALL CLEAR" is issued by SDMA. Avoid drinking contaminated coastal tap water.
