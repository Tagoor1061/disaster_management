IMD Rainfall Warning Scale
  • Light Rain (15.6 – 64.4 mm): Normal monsoon conditions; slight drainage runoff.
  • Heavy Rain (64.5 – 115.5 mm): Yellow/Orange warning; potential low-lying waterlogging.
  • Very Heavy Rain (115.6 – 204.4 mm): Orange/Red warning; municipal de-watering active.
  • Extremely Heavy Rain (>204.5 mm): Red alert; risk of severe urban inundation.
Stormwater Drainage Protocols
  • Heavy duty de-watering pumps deployed at major underpasses and low-lying residential wards.
  • Automated trash-rack clearing across major city drains and culverts.
  • Continuous monitoring of Guntur Canal inflow and discharge rates.
  • Emergency sandbag bunding pre-positioned near vulnerable wards.

Flood & Heavy Rain Survival Actions

🚶 Move to Higher Ground

If water enters your street or home, move immediately to higher floors or designated relief shelters. Never walk or drive through moving floodwater — 15 cm of fast water can knock an adult down.

🎒 Prepare an Emergency Kit

Keep drinking water, dry food, torch, power bank, first-aid, essential medicines, radio, whistle and documents in a waterproof bag — ready to grab in 5 minutes.

🔌 Disconnect Electrical Appliances

Switch off mains and unplug appliances before water reaches sockets. Never touch electrical switches with wet hands; report snapped wires to the Electricity Emergency 1912.

🚗 Avoid Flooded Routes

Do not enter waterlogged underpasses, culverts or canal banks. Turn around — don't drown. Park vehicles on elevated ground away from trees and hoardings.

📢 Follow Official Alerts

Track IMD Red/Orange/Yellow warnings on this page and enable push alerts. Cooperate with municipal de-watering and rescue teams.

💧 After the Rain

Boil drinking water, avoid contact with stagnant water (electrocution & disease risk), report open manholes and clogged drains via this portal immediately.

Extreme Rainfall Safety & Preparedness Steps

1. Before Heavy Downpour

Clean terrace outlets and household storm drains. Keep emergency torches, power banks, and essential dry provisions ready.

2. During Torrential Rain

Avoid driving through flooded underpasses or open drains. Stay clear of submerged electric poles and transformers.

3. After Rainfall Subsides

Boil drinking water before consumption. Report waterlogging, open manholes, or clogged drains immediately via our portal.
