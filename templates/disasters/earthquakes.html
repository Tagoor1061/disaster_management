Earthquake Survival Actions (DROP, COVER, HOLD)

1. DROP to the Ground

Drop onto your hands and knees immediately to prevent being knocked over by sudden ground shaking.

2. COVER Your Head & Neck

Take cover under a sturdy desk or table. If no shelter is nearby, cover head and neck with your arms against an interior wall.

3. HOLD ON Until Shaking Stops

Hold on to your shelter until shaking ceases. After shaking stops, exit carefully using stairs (NEVER use elevators).
