Disaster Safety Guidelines for Citizens

DO's During Floods & Storms

  • Move to higher ground or assigned flood relief shelters.
  • Keep emergency battery lights, power banks, and flashlights handy.
  • Drink only boiled or purified bottled water.
  • Report drainage overflow or fallen trees immediately via our portal.

DON'Ts During Disasters

  • Do not walk or drive through flowing flood waters.
  • Avoid touching loose electric wires or submerged poles.
  • Do not spread unverified emergency rumors on social media.
  • Do not ignore official municipal evacuation advisories.
