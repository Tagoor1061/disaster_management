About Suraksha Kavach

Department of Government Municipal Corporation — Dedicated to civic safety, waterlogging control, emergency flood response, and public welfare in Guntur and across Andhra Pradesh.

🎯 Our Mission

Suraksha Kavach — "the shield that guards every life" — is a modern municipal initiative aimed at protecting citizens from every natural hazard: cyclones, tsunamis, floods, earthquakes, high winds, extreme rainfall and landslides. We empower citizens with AI-powered early warnings, real-time reporting tools, live weather updates, interactive geographic mapping, and rapid administrative response.

🏛️ Municipal Excellence

Guntur Municipal Corporation strives to combine technology with public administration to build resilient urban infrastructure. Through our platform, officials efficiently track, prioritize, and resolve community issue reports in real time.
